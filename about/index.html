About Easel Ultimate

A free, browser-based CNC design app inspired by Inventables Easel. It recreates the design workflow — board setup, shapes, text, V-carving, pockets, simulation, and G-code export — and unlocks the features that usually sit behind a Pro subscription.

Everything included all free

🕳️ Pockets — offset & raster fill
✏️ V-Carve text with V-bits
⛰️ Multi-pass with ramping
𝕋 Text engraving (5 fonts)
✎ Freehand pen tool
◈ Design library & pattern generators
📷 Image trace to pockets
📐 Measure tool
⚙️ Machine profiles (X-Carve → custom GRBL)
📚 Projects with live thumbnails
🎬 Carve simulation with progress
⏱ Carve time estimate
⬇ Real GRBL G-code export
🖼 PNG export
📦 Shareable .easel design files
👤 Local accounts & notes

Design philosophy

Your designs belong to you. Everything is stored in your own browser — no accounts to sync, no cloud, no tracking, no subscription. Open the app on any device with a browser and it just works, even offline (save the page and open it later).
