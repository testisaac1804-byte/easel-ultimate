Help

Quick start

  1. Create a project — pick your machine, material, and board size.
  2. Design — add shapes from the toolbar: rectangles, circles, text, pen drawings, library shapes, or trace a photo.
  3. Check the cut — the right panel shows the carve time. Press ▶ Simulate to watch the carve.
  4. Carve — press Carve…, review the G-code, then Download .gcode and send it with UGS or Candle.

Tips from the pros

  • Keep depth per pass at or below half the bit diameter for wood.
  • Use V-carve text for elegant lettering — a 60°/90° V-bit gives sharp corners.
  • Always clamp or tape the board to your spoilboard.
  • Run the simulation before carving — it catches most mistakes.
  • For signs, add a trim line (a shallow rect) around the text so you know where to cut the board.

Sharing designs

Press Share → Save design file to get a .easel file, then email it. The receiver opens it with Project → Open design file in the app. You can also copy the link to this site — everyone can use the app in their own browser.

Opening a gallery design

  1. Download the .easel file from the gallery.
  2. In the app: Project → Open design file (.easel)… and pick the file.
  3. It appears on your home screen as an imported project.

G-code & machines

The app writes standard GRBL G-code (G17/G21/G90, M3 spindle, negative-Z cuts). It works with X-Carve, Shapeoko, Onefinity, OpenBuilds, and any GRBL controller. Send it with UGS, Candle, or any GRBL sender.

Privacy

Everything is saved in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Accounts, projects, and designs never leave your device.
