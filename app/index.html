Quick tips

        
    
          
  • Always wear eye & ear protection when carving.
    • 
          
  • Keep depth per pass at or below half the bit diameter for wood.
    • 
          
  • Clamp the board to the spoilboard — double-sided tape works great.
    • 
          
  • Run the simulation before carving: it catches most issues.
    • 
          
  • Send the .gcode with UGS or Candle (both free).
